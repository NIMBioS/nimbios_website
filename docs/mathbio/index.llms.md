# Mathematical Biology at NIMBioS

For decades, work at the interface of mathematics and biology has been core to the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. NIMBioS grew out of this tradition, resulting in discoveries in areas ranging from disease ecology to paleontology. That work continues; below are some recent publications by NIMBioS [affiliate](affiliates/index.llms.md) faculty. NIMBioS has also fostered tens of millions of dollars of federal investment in research (current active grants (as of October, 2026) total over \$20M), resulting in new innovations and publications, and, perhaps even more importantly, training of dozens of scientists as grad students and postdocs.

Historically, one of NIMBioS’ biggest impacts has been through working groups and catalysis meetings. Groups of researchers would propose a synthetic topic and, if approved, NIMBioS would support one or more visits from researchers around the world to Knoxville to work through this topic. These topics would commonly span various areas of mathematics, biology, and computing; a key skill was for participants to be able to translate questions and jargon across domains.
